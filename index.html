ABOUT THE PROJECT

Small stages. Wider circles.

Houselights helps independent theatre teams discover aggregate cultural affinities and turn them into outreach hypotheses. Qloo provides the connections; Gemini chooses tools and interprets the evidence. Affinity is not a probability that someone will buy a ticket. Verify places, permissions, and dates before acting.

No messages are sent and nothing is published. Demographic estimates are not inferred. Created for the Qloo Agentic Hackathon by WooYoung Choi, with AI coding assistance.
